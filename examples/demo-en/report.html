Method and limitations

1) Markdown policies and evidence are loaded and split into chunks. 2) The most relevant chunks are retrieved for each control (BM25 + document tags). 3) Deterministic checks: review dates, owners, drafts, superseded documents, SoA, commitment reconciliation and rule tests on the evidence tables. 4) Control assessment (heuristic or Claude). 5) A control can never be 'met' if it relies on an expired or draft document or has a missed commitment or a failed rule. 6) Findings are grouped by root cause to detect patterns.
